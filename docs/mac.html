Teaching guide:
the Mac build

Eight sittings, in order, each one ending in a check that either passes or fails. Written to be taught from: the left column says how long and who does it.

For Apple Silicon Macs Shell zsh Total hands-on about 4 hours Spread over 2 or 3 evenings

The one thing that is different on a Mac

Get this wrong and he spends a night watching a progress bar that was never going to finish.

Everything in this build runs on a Mac except one step: training the singing voice model. RVC training does not properly use the Apple GPU. It silently falls back to the CPU, and people report six hours or more for a thirty minute dataset on an M1 Pro.

So the split is: train once in the cloud, run every day on the Mac. Training happens on a free Google Colab GPU in about an hour. The trained voice is two small files he downloads and keeps forever. Daily conversion then runs locally in seconds.

Do not let him try to train locally

It will appear to be working. It starts, it prints epochs, it never meaningfully progresses. This is the single most likely way for him to give up on the project in week one.

Find out which Mac he has

5 minutesHim, on a call

Apple menu, About This Mac. Two numbers matter: the chip and the memory. Everything after this branches on them.

What he hasMusic generationVoice conversionPlan
Intel Mac Not viableNot viable Everything in Colab, Mac is just for editing and upload. Skip sessions 6 and 7.
M-series, 8 GB Will run out of memoryFine Music in Colab, conversion and video locally.
M-series, 16 GB Yes, small modelFine Everything local. Use the 0.6B language model, not the bigger ones.
M2 Pro or better, 32 GB+ Yes, any modelFast Everything local, no compromises.

For reference on speed: an M2 Max renders roughly 26 seconds of compute per minute of finished audio, so a 3 and a half minute song lands in about a minute and a half. An M1 Max is close behind.

Checkpoint

He can tell you the chip name and the memory figure. Write both down before continuing.

1

Foundations: Terminal, Homebrew, ffmpeg

30 minutesScreen share

Open Terminal (Command+Space, type "Terminal"). Everything below is typed there. His shell is zsh, which is the modern Mac default.

Step 1a · Xcode command line tools

Nothing else installs without these. A dialog box will appear, click Install, then wait.

xcode-select --install

If it says "already installed", that is a pass, move on.

Step 1b · Homebrew

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"
The step everyone misses

On Apple Silicon, Homebrew installs to /opt/homebrew, which is not on the path by default. At the end of the install it prints two lines under "Next steps". Those lines must actually be run. If they are skipped, every later command fails with "command not found" and it looks like the install failed when it did not. 

echo 'eval "$(/opt/homebrew/bin/brew shellenv)"' >> ~/.zprofile
eval "$(/opt/homebrew/bin/brew shellenv)"

Step 1c · The three tools everything else needs

brew install python@3.12 ffmpeg git

ffmpeg is the video renderer. It is a 300 MB install with its dependencies, so this takes a few minutes.

Checkpoint

All three of these print a version number rather than "command not found":

$ brew --version
Homebrew 4.x.x
$ python3 --version
Python 3.12.x
$ ffmpeg -version
ffmpeg version 7.x.x ...
If "command not found" appears anyway

Close Terminal completely (Command+Q, not just the window) and reopen it. The ~/.zprofile line only takes effect in a new session. If it still fails, run cat ~/.zprofile and confirm the brew line is actually in there.

Prove the Gospel feed works

10 minutesScreen share

This needs no GPU and no AI. Do it early because it is a guaranteed win and it makes the whole thing feel real. 

mkdir -p ~/gospel-song && cd ~/gospel-song

Put gospel.py in that folder (send it to him, or paste it in with nano gospel.py, then Control+O, Return, Control+X to save and exit). Then: 

python3 gospel.py --out hoy.json
Checkpoint

He sees today's Gospel citation and the commentary author, in Spanish:

$ python3 gospel.py --out hoy.json
2026-08-15 [SP] -> hoy.json
  gospel     Lc 1,39-56.  (1523 chars)

If it fails with a certificate error, that is antivirus software intercepting HTTPS. Run pip3 install truststore and try again. The script picks it up automatically.

3

Set up the Grok automation

15 minutesHim, alone

No installing. In the Grok app or on grok.com: Automations, new task, paste the Spanish prompt from the pipeline plan, set it to run daily at 05:00 in his timezone, delivery by email.

This needs a paid Grok tier, either X Premium+ or a grok.com subscription. Free accounts do not get Automations.

Then stop and wait three days

Do not install anything else yet. Let it email him for three mornings and read the lyrics properly. If the songs are not good, the problem is the prompt, and fixing a prompt costs nothing while fixing a prompt after building a pipeline around it costs a weekend.

Checkpoint

Three emails arrive on three consecutive mornings, each with all seven ===SECTION=== blocks present.

4

Record the voice dataset

45 minutesHim, alone

This is the one step where the quality of his effort decides the quality of the final result, and it only has to happen once. He is not performing, he is giving the model material.

What to record

  • 10 to 30 minutes of him singing. Singing, not talking. The model learns his timbre across his pitch range, and a speech-only dataset produces a thin, strained sound on high notes.
  • Solo and dry. No backing track, no reverb, no autotune, no room echo. If a backing track bleeds in, the model learns the backing track too.
  • Cover his whole range. Low verses and high choruses. Whatever he never records, the model has to guess.
  • Same mic, same room, one sitting. Consistency beats quality here. A decent USB mic or even AirPods in a quiet room beats a great mic in three different rooms.

How to record it

GarageBand is already on his Mac and is more than enough. New project, Voice track, turn every effect and preset off, record. Export with Share, Export Song to Disk, and choose WAV, not MP3.

Then cut it into clips

cd ~/gospel-song
mkdir -p dataset
ffmpeg -i canto_crudo.wav -f segment -segment_time 10 \
  -ac 1 -ar 44100 -c:a pcm_s16le dataset/voz_%03d.wav

Ten second slices, mono, 44.1 kHz. Afterwards he should delete any clip that is silence, coughing, or a false start.

Checkpoint

ls dataset | wc -l shows at least 60 files, and playing three at random gives clean solo singing with no background noise.

5

Train the voice in Colab, not on the Mac

20 minutes hands-on, then about an hour of waitingScreen share

Zip the dataset folder, upload it to his Google Drive, then open the Applio Colab notebook from the Applio repository. It runs on Google's free T4 GPU. 

cd ~/gospel-song
zip -r dataset.zip dataset

In the notebook

  1. Runtime, Change runtime type, select T4 GPU. Do this before running any cell.
  2. Run the install cell. It takes a few minutes.
  3. Mount Drive and point the training cell at dataset.zip.
  4. Name the model something he will recognise in a year, not "test1".
  5. Sample rate 40k, around 300 epochs, save every 50.
  6. Train. Check back in an hour.
Free Colab disconnects

Free sessions get reclaimed if the tab is idle, so he should leave it open and touch it occasionally. This is exactly why saving every 50 epochs matters: a disconnect at epoch 240 costs him one checkpoint, not the whole run.

What to download when it finishes

Two files, and they are the entire point of this session: a .pth and an .index. Put them in ~/gospel-song/voz/ on the Mac and back them up somewhere. That pair is his voice, reusable forever, and regenerating it means redoing the recording session.

Checkpoint

Both files sit in ~/gospel-song/voz/ and the .pth is somewhere in the region of 50 to 200 MB. A file of a few kilobytes means the training did not actually complete.

6

Install the two local apps

30 minutes, plus a long downloadScreen share

Skip this session entirely if he is on an Intel Mac or 8 GB. Use Colab for music instead.

Applio, for daily voice conversion

cd ~
git clone https://github.com/IAHispano/Applio.git
cd Applio
chmod +x run-install.sh run-applio.sh
./run-install.sh

Then to start it, now and every time after:

cd ~/Applio && ./run-applio.sh

A browser tab opens with the interface. He loads the .pth and .index from session 5 under Inference.

ACE-Step 1.5, for the music

curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh
cd ~
git clone https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5.git
cd ACE-Step-1.5
uv sync
chmod +x start_gradio_ui_macos.sh
./start_gradio_ui_macos.sh

Use the macos script specifically. It sets Apple's MLX backend for the language model and PyTorch's Metal backend for the audio model. The plain start_gradio_ui.sh assumes an NVIDIA card and will fail.

First launch downloads several gigabytes of model weights. Start it and go and do something else. The interface then opens at http://localhost:7860.

On a 16 GB Mac, pick the small language model

There is a choice of 0.6B, 1.7B, and 4B. The 0.6B runs without running out of memory on any M-series Mac with 16 GB. The larger ones will crash it.

If a memory error appears anyway, adding PYTORCH_MPS_HIGH_WATERMARK_RATIO=0.0 removes the allocation ceiling, but it also removes a protection that stops a runaway process taking down macOS itself. Try the smaller model first.

Checkpoint

Both interfaces open in a browser, and ACE-Step produces a listenable 30 second test clip from any prompt.

If macOS blocks something as unverified

Right-click the file and choose Open rather than double-clicking it, or go to System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click "Open Anyway" next to the blocked item. Gatekeeper stops unsigned downloads by default.

7

The daily loop

About 15 minutes a dayHim, alone

Once, slowly, together. Then it is his.

  1. Read the email. Change at least one line of the lyric so it sounds like him. This is not optional, and session 8 explains why.
  2. ACE-Step. Paste the ===LETRA=== block into lyrics and the ===ESTILO=== block into style. Generate three takes and keep the best one.
  3. Split the vocal from the music. 
    cd ~/gospel-song
python3 -m demucs --two-stems=vocals cancion.wav
  4. Applio. Convert vocals.wav using his voice model. Output voz_clonada.wav.
  5. Mix it back together. 
    ffmpeg -i separated/htdemucs/cancion/no_vocals.wav -i voz_clonada.wav \
  -filter_complex "[1:a]volume=1.15[v];[0:a][v]amix=inputs=2:duration=longest" \
  -c:a pcm_s16le final.wav
  6. Record a spoken intro in his real voice. Twenty to thirty seconds. Voice Memos is fine.
  7. Time the lyrics and render the video. 
    pip3 install whisperx
whisperx final.wav --language es --model medium --output_format ass

ffmpeg -loop 1 -i fondo.jpg -i final.wav \
  -vf "scale=3840:-2,\
zoompan=z='min(zoom+0.00015,1.18)':d=25*215:\
x='iw/2-(iw/zoom/2)':y='ih/2-(ih/zoom/2)':s=1920x1080:fps=25,\
subtitles=final.ass" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest salida.mp4
  8. Upload from youtube.com, and tick the synthetic content box.

WhisperX runs on the CPU on a Mac, because the library underneath it has no Metal support. For a three minute song that is a couple of minutes of waiting, and its word timings are noticeably better than the faster Apple-native alternatives. For a lyric video, accuracy is worth more than speed.

d=25*215 is the pan length in frames: 25 frames per second times the song length in seconds. He should change 215 to match the actual song or the pan finishes early and freezes.

Checkpoint

One finished MP4, three to four minutes long, that plays with his voice and lyrics on screen in time.

8

The rules that keep the channel alive

10 minutesSay this out loud to him

This is the part to be blunt about, because the tooling is the easy half and this is where channels like his actually die.

RuleWhat happens if ignoredWhat he does
Disclose synthetic content Demonetisation on its own Tick "altered or synthetic content" on every upload. It costs nothing: no reach penalty, no ad penalty.
Do not mass-produce Warning, then 90 day suspension, then permanent removal from the Partner Program Real spoken intro plus one hand-edited lyric line, every single day.
Never sing the translation Copyright claim on the audio Original paraphrase only. Cite the passage in the description instead.
Why the two minutes of real work matter

A fully automatic daily AI song channel is the exact thing YouTube's inauthentic content policy was written to catch. The intro and the edited line are what make it AI-assisted work with human direction, which is explicitly allowed. Build the habit on day one, because adding it after a strike does not undo the strike.

Teaching order, if you only have one evening

These four get him to something he can hear. The rest can wait a week.

Do these together

  • Session 0, five minutes, decides everything else
  • Session 1, foundations, he cannot do this one alone
  • Session 2, the Gospel feed, an early guaranteed win
  • Session 3, Grok, delivers value before anything is installed

Leave him with

  • Session 4, the recording, entirely his own effort
  • Three mornings of Grok emails to read critically
  • One instruction: do not try to train the voice on the Mac

Then meet again for sessions 5 and 6, which are the ones that genuinely need two people.
